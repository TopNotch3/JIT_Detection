# Business Model Hypotheses

## Status

The course proposal does not define a commercial business model. This document is therefore a hypothesis framework, not a validated business plan.

## Potential customer

Software engineering organizations that need to prioritize review attention and identify risky changes earlier.

## Potential value

- Earlier identification of risky changes.
- Better prioritization of code review effort.
- Visibility into repository-level risk.
- Explainable risk indicators.

## Potential delivery models

### Cloud SaaS
Potentially easiest to deploy, but repository privacy requirements must be addressed.

### Self-hosted
Potentially attractive for organizations with strict source-code controls.

### Hybrid
Sensitive source processing could remain within the customer's environment.

## Potential pricing dimensions

To validate:
- per developer;
- per repository;
- per organization;
- usage-based;
- enterprise license.

## ROI hypothesis

The business case should eventually quantify:
- review time saved;
- defects caught before release;
- avoided remediation cost;
- reduced escaped defects.

No monetary ROI should be claimed until supported by measured or sourced evidence.
